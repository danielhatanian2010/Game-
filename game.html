HOW TO PLAY

Goal: eliminate every enemy in the sector, then reach the glowing EXTRACT pad.

Move: touch and drag anywhere on the left of the screen — a joystick appears.

Takedown ✦: sneak up on an enemy and tap. It only works when the enemy can't see you — stay out of their cone.

Grapple / Web-Zip ⇱: fire toward a wall to zip across the map. Warden and Nightfall grapple; Weaver web-zips.

Ability ◎: Radar Sense, Smoke Screen, or Web Trap — hero-specific.

Weapon ➤: Warden's ricocheting billy clubs, Nightfall's returning batarang, or Weaver's web shot. Watch the cooldown ring.

Stealth: stay out of the yellow vision cones and out of the light. Cones turn orange when suspicious and red when you're spotted. Get shot and your HP drops — lose it all and the mission fails.

Sound: footsteps and loud takedowns can be heard. Move deliberately.

Desktop: WASD move, J takedown, Space dash, K ability, L gadget.
